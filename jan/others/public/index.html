From Problem to Solution

The 5-Stage Innovation Lifecycle

A transparent, accountable workflow connecting grassroots identification to scalable industrial deployment.

1
📱
Citizen Submission
Citizens, NGOs, or local bodies submit real problems with photos, GPS coordinates, priority, and impact description.
2
🤖
AI Triage & Review
Natural Language Processing automatically categorizes the problem, detects duplicates, and assigns priority. Admins validate.
3
🏛️
HEI Assignment
Validated challenges are assigned to relevant university departments (IITs, NITs, Central & State HEIs) for R&D.
4
🏭
Industry & CSR Co-Creation
Industry partners provide technical mentorship, CSR funding, specialized equipment, and pilot infrastructure.
5
🚀
Field Pilot & Deployment
Solutions undergo rigorous field testing in affected communities. Citizen feedback confirms resolution before sign-off.

About JanSetu

Solving India's Toughest Challenges Through Synergy

Communities across India encounter critical issues in water access, rural education, sustainable farming, and urban infrastructure. While citizens are the first to identify these issues, there was no structured mechanism to funnel them to innovators.

JanSetu provides an end-to-end platform where problems are verified with AI, assigned to academic research labs, co-funded by industry CSR, and tracked through deployment with community accountability.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participating in the innovation ecosystem.

Who can submit a challenge on JanSetu? +
Any Indian citizen, community representative, village head, NGO, or municipal body can register for free and submit societal challenges with description and photo/document evidence.
How do universities get involved? +
Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) register university accounts, map their departments (Engineering, Agriculture, Public Health, Social Sciences), and receive curated problem statements matched to their domain expertise.
How does industry and CSR funding work? +
Industry partners can browse assigned challenges, pledge CSR funding, provide specialized technology or mentorship, and partner with university labs to co-develop production-grade solutions.
Is there AI duplicate detection and categorization? +
Yes! The platform includes built-in Natural Language Processing that classifies problems into 10 domains and flags duplicate submissions from nearby GPS coordinates.
